Workflow Orchestration

Workflow orchestration is the system design around the model: chains, routers, parallel workers, managers, tools, judges, and verifiers.

Ordinary Problem

Real tasks are too large or varied for one undifferentiated model call.

Naive Picture

One large prompt should handle planning, tools, checking, and synthesis.

Why It Fails

Different subtasks need different routes, tools, budgets, and checks.

First-Principles Move

Expose the workflow structure. Once steps are explicit, they can be routed, parallelized, verified, retried, and debugged.

What Breaks Without It

The agent becomes opaque: you cannot tell where it failed or how to improve it.

Course Role

Part 1 introduces orchestration patterns that later lectures deepen.
