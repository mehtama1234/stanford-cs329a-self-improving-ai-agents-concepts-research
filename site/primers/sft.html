Supervised Finetuning

Supervised finetuning trains a model on examples of desired input-output behavior.

First Principles

After pretraining learns broad representations, SFT changes how the model responds to a prompt. It shows the model what kind of answer should follow: concise answer, chain of reasoning, tool call, JSON format, safety refusal, or domain-specific response.

Concrete Example

A dataset of customer support questions and ideal responses teaches the model to answer in the company's support style.

Why It Matters

SFT is the easiest way to teach format, style, task behavior, and teacher-model reasoning traces. Distillation is often implemented as SFT on synthetic outputs from a stronger model.

Common Misunderstanding

SFT is not only about making a model polite. It can transfer procedural traces and sometimes install reasoning patterns the base model did not naturally use.
