# Extraction Rubric

A concept is accepted only if it answers a real conceptual job in the lecture.

Required fields:

- `ordinary_problem`: the human or engineering problem before jargon
- `naive_picture`: the tempting simple view
- `why_naive_fails`: what that view misses
- `first_principles`: the underlying mechanism
- `what_breaks_without_it`: the failure mode
- `evidence_ids`: at least one transcript-backed evidence anchor

Evidence labels:

- `direct transcript support`: the lecture says or demonstrates the claim
- `inference from transcript`: the claim follows from several lecture spans
- `synthesis`: the package interpretation; useful, but not directly said
- `external context`: comes from outside the transcript corpus
