Rationale Bootstrapping

Rationale bootstrapping uses generated or curated explanations to help a model learn better reasoning behavior.

Ordinary Problem

You may have answers but not enough high-quality explanations showing how to reach them.

Naive Picture

Only final-answer examples are needed for reasoning tasks.

Why It Fails

For hard reasoning, the path matters. A model trained only on answers may not learn the intermediate structure.

First-Principles Move

A rationale is process data. If it is verified or filtered, it can teach the model the shape of successful reasoning.

What Breaks Without It

The model learns answer patterns without learning the method.

Course Role

Part 6 introduces STaR and rationales as train-time reasoning improvement methods.
