RLHF

RLHF means reinforcement learning from human feedback, usually used to align model outputs with human preferences.

First Principles

Humans compare or rate model outputs. A reward model learns those preferences. The language model is then optimized to produce outputs the reward model scores highly.

Concrete Example

Given two answers, humans prefer the answer that is more helpful and less evasive. The model is trained to increase the probability of answers like that.

Why It Matters

RLHF made chat models more usable, but it can also produce over-optimization, sycophancy, mode collapse, or responses that look good to the reward model without being truly better.

Common Misunderstanding

RLHF is not the same as RLVR. RLHF uses human preference signals; RLVR uses automatically checkable correctness signals.
