Deep Research Agents

Deep research agents search across sources, decide what is relevant, synthesize evidence, and produce a structured answer or report.

Ordinary Problem

Research work is not one lookup. It requires source discovery, relevance filtering, synthesis, citation, and uncertainty handling.

Naive Picture

Deep research is just summarizing more documents after collecting a larger pile of sources.

Why It Fails

The hard part is deciding what to search, what to trust, how to combine sources, and when enough evidence has been gathered.

First-Principles Move

Research is an agent loop over information: ask, search, read, update beliefs, search again, synthesize, and cite.

What Breaks Without It

The report becomes a fluent collage with weak source control.

Course Role

Parts 1, 7, and 9 repeatedly use deep research as a central agent workflow.
