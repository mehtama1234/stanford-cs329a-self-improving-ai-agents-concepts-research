RL Information Inefficiency

RL information inefficiency means RL often gives very little learning signal per unit of compute, especially on long tasks with sparse final rewards.

First Principles

Pretraining gives token-level feedback on every token. RL may generate thousands or millions of tokens before receiving one success/failure bit. If the pass rate is near zero or near one, even that bit carries little useful information.

Concrete Example

A model works for an hour on a coding task and only learns that the final solution failed. It does not automatically know which step caused the failure.

Why It Matters

This explains why curriculum design, process rewards, verifiers, DAPO-style filtering, and task generation near the model's frontier are so important.

Common Misunderstanding

RL being inefficient does not mean RL is useless. It means the reward bits are expensive and must be aimed carefully.
