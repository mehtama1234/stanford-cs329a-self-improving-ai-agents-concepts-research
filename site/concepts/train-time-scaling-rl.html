Train-Time Scaling With RL

Train-time scaling improves the model itself using rationales, rewards, verifiers, and reinforcement learning instead of only spending more compute at inference.

Ordinary Problem

Inference tricks can help a fixed model, but you may want the model's default behavior to improve over time.

Naive Picture

Self-improvement only happens during runtime search after the model has already been trained.

Why It Fails

Runtime successes and verifier feedback can become training data or rewards that change future model behavior.

First-Principles Move

Feedback can be used in two places: at inference to select an answer, or during training to shift the model's policy toward better reasoning trajectories.

What Breaks Without It

You repeatedly pay for the same correction at inference time instead of teaching the model to avoid it.

Course Role

Part 6 connects reasoning rationales, DeepSeek-style math training, and RL for long chains.
