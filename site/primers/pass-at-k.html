Pass@1 And Pass@k

Pass@1 measures whether the first answer is correct. Pass@k measures whether at least one of k sampled answers is correct.

First Principles

Single-shot accuracy measures reliability under one draw. Multi-shot accuracy measures whether the model's distribution contains a correct solution somewhere within repeated attempts.

Concrete Example

A model with 40% pass@1 but 90% pass@100 is unreliable but capable. A model with 70% pass@1 and 75% pass@100 is reliable but less diverse.

Why It Matters

The gap between pass@1 and pass@k reveals whether training improved reliability, expanded capability, or collapsed diversity.

Common Misunderstanding

Higher pass@1 does not always mean the model has a broader reasoning boundary. It may simply sample a narrower set of good common paths.
