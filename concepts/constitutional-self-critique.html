Constitutional Self-Critique

Constitutional self-critique uses model-generated critique against a set of principles to improve or filter outputs.

Ordinary Problem

Some tasks do not have a simple executable test, but still need feedback about quality, safety, or policy alignment.

Naive Picture

Only humans can provide useful preference or critique data.

Why It Fails

Human feedback is expensive and slow; model critique can scale certain forms of review when grounded by a rubric.

First-Principles Move

A principle set acts like a soft verifier. It cannot prove correctness, but it can create structured feedback when objective checks are unavailable.

What Breaks Without It

The system lacks scalable feedback for subjective or policy-heavy outputs.

Course Role

Part 4 includes constitutional AI as a feedback method alongside tool and execution feedback.
