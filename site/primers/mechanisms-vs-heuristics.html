Mechanisms Versus Heuristics

Mechanisms are reasoning moves the model can perform; heuristics decide when to use them.

First Principles

A model may already contain mechanisms like calculation, restatement, checking, decomposition, and backtracking. Training may primarily improve the internal decision (the control policy) about when to activate these mechanisms.

Concrete Example

The model already knows how to verify an equation, but RL teaches it to verify before finalizing the answer on math problems.

Why It Matters

This distinction clarifies claims that base models know how to reason while thinking models learn when to reason.

Common Misunderstanding

A behavior becoming more frequent after RL does not prove the mechanism was newly created by RL.
