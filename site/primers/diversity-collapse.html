Diversity Collapse

Diversity collapse happens when training makes a model repeat a narrower set of output patterns, even if those patterns are often good.

First Principles

RL increases probability mass on rewarded trajectories. If the reward does not explicitly preserve variety, the model may over-concentrate on common winning strategies and suppress rare but useful alternatives.

Concrete Example

After RL, a model may solve common algebra problems more reliably but stop trying unusual proof strategies that occasionally solved hard problems.

Why It Matters

This explains why RL models can improve pass@1 while hurting pass@k. It also matters for creative, open-ended, and out-of-distribution tasks.

Common Misunderstanding

Collapse is not always bad. For safety or routine tasks, narrowing the output distribution can be useful. The problem is losing valuable rare reasoning paths.
