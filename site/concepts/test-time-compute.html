inference

Test-Time Compute

Test-time compute means spending extra computation while using a fixed model to improve the final answer.

Ordinary Problem

A model may know how to solve a problem sometimes, but one attempt may land on a weak path.

Naive Picture

One model call measures the model's full ability.

Why It Fails

Generation is variable. A single answer may miss correct paths that appear under repeated attempts, search, or refinement.

First-Principles Move

If the model's answer distribution contains good candidates, then more inference can sample or search that distribution. The gain appears only if the system can choose or verify better candidates.

What Breaks Without It

You underuse capable models or waste runtime compute without a selection rule.

Course Role

Part 2 develops this as a major self-improvement mechanism.
