QK-Norm

QK-norm normalizes query and key vectors in attention to improve training stability.

First Principles

Attention scores come from interactions between query and key vectors. If their magnitudes become unstable, attention can become too sharp, too noisy, or prone to training spikes. Normalization controls the scale of those interactions.

Concrete Example

Instead of letting query-key dot products grow without control, qk-norm keeps the attention comparison in a more stable range.

Why It Matters

Stable attention is especially important for large models, long contexts, and MoE systems where instability can compound.

Common Misunderstanding

QK-norm is not a reasoning method by itself. It is an architectural stabilization method that can support better reasoning indirectly.
