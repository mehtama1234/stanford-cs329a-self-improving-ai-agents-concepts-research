Search-Space Curation

Search-space curation is the work of sampling many possible outputs and filtering, clustering, testing, or ranking them into a final answer.

Ordinary Problem

The model's output space may contain good solutions, but most samples may be redundant, weak, or wrong.

Naive Picture

If the right answer is somewhere in the samples, we are done.

Why It Fails

A large candidate set still requires deduplication, selection, verification, and sometimes diversity control.

First-Principles Move

Search creates raw possibility. Curation turns possibility into a usable decision by imposing tests and selection criteria.

What Breaks Without It

The system drowns in candidates and cannot reliably extract the correct one.
