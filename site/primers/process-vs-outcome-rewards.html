Process Rewards Versus Outcome Rewards

Outcome rewards score the final answer. Process rewards score intermediate reasoning steps.

First Principles

Many wrong solutions look plausible until the end, and some correct answers come from invalid reasoning. Outcome rewards are cheap when final correctness is checkable, but process rewards can provide denser guidance about where reasoning went right or wrong.

Concrete Example

For a math proof, an outcome reward checks the final answer. A process reward checks each algebraic transformation.

Why It Matters

Process rewards can reduce error propagation and improve training signal density, but they are harder to build and easier to miscalibrate.

Common Misunderstanding

A process reward is not automatically more truthful. A bad process judge can reward polished but invalid reasoning.
