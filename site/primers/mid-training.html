Mid-Training

Mid-training is an intermediate training phase between broad pretraining and final post-training, often used to specialize the model toward important domains or long-context behavior.

First Principles

Pretraining gives general capability. Final post-training shapes interaction behavior. Mid-training changes the base distribution with targeted data such as code, math, multilingual data, long-context documents, or domain corpora before alignment and RL.

Concrete Example

A model is pretrained on broad web text, then mid-trained on high-quality code and math, then instruction-tuned and RL-trained.

Why It Matters

Mid-training is one reason modern models can improve substantially without obvious architectural changes.

Common Misunderstanding

Mid-training is not just more pretraining. Its data mix and objective are chosen to move the model toward desired capabilities.
