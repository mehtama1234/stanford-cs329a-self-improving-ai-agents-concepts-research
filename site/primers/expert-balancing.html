Expert Balancing

Expert balancing keeps an MoE model from overusing a few experts while ignoring others.

First Principles

If the router always chooses the same experts, unused experts do not learn well and busy experts become bottlenecks. Balancing methods encourage healthier distribution of tokens across experts.

Concrete Example

During training, a bias or auxiliary objective can push the router away from overloaded experts and toward underused ones.

Why It Matters

Poor balancing wastes capacity and can destabilize reasoning because similar prompts may route through inconsistent expert paths.

Common Misunderstanding

Expert balancing is not only a hardware efficiency trick. It affects what experts learn and how stable routing becomes.
