ReAct: Reason, Act, Observe

ReAct is a pattern where the model alternates between thinking about what to do, taking an action, observing the result, and continuing.

Ordinary Problem

A multi-step task needs both reasoning and new information from actions.

Naive Picture

Reasoning and acting are separate phases that can be planned once and then executed.

Why It Fails

Actions change what the model knows. Reasoning must adapt after every observation.

First-Principles Move

The agent's state is updated by observations. A good next action depends on both the original goal and the latest observed result.

What Breaks Without It

The model plans blindly and fails to revise when the environment responds differently than expected.

Course Role

Part 4 presents ReAct as an early tool-augmented agent pattern.
