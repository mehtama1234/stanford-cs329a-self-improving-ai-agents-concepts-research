Verifiers

A verifier is a system that checks whether an answer, step, action, or trajectory is correct or acceptable.

First Principles

Generation creates candidates; verification selects among them or turns them into training signal. The verifier can be symbolic, executable, rule-based, learned, or another language model.

Concrete Example

Unit tests verify code. A theorem checker verifies a proof. A rubric-based judge checks whether a support answer follows policy.

Why It Matters

Verifiers are the bridge between sampling and learning. Without verification, more samples may just produce more noise.

Common Misunderstanding

An LLM judge is not automatically a reliable verifier. It needs calibration, adversarial testing, and fallback paths.
