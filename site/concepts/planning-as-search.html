planning

Planning As Search

Planning as search treats an agent's future as a tree or graph of possible thoughts, actions, observations, and next states.

Ordinary Problem

The first plan may be wrong, and many possible next actions compete for attention.

Naive Picture

Generate one complete plan from start to finish and then follow it.

Why It Fails

Long tasks need branching, backtracking, and updating as information arrives.

First-Principles Move

Planning chooses among possible trajectories. Search lets the agent explore alternatives, score partial paths, and commit only after enough evidence.

What Breaks Without It

The agent becomes brittle: one early bad choice corrupts the rest of the task.

Course Role

Part 5 centers this through Language Agent Tree Search.
